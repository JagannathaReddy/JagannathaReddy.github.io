Hi, I'm Jagannatha. For most of my career I've built the plumbing underneath other people's test suites: page-object scaffolds, API test frameworks, reporters, the CI wiring nobody notices until it breaks.

These days I build what comes after that plumbing: agents that write, run and repair Playwright tests, and that are allowed to say no when a "fix" would just hide a real bug. Same instinct as always. Don't trust an assertion until you've watched it fail, and watched the fix pass.

BIO FOR EVENTS & INTROS

Jagannatha MV is an SDET at Signify who builds AI agents for test automation. He created AgenticPw, an AI teammate for Playwright suites, and luma-browser, a sandboxed CLI for driving real browsers from plain JavaScript.

Jagannatha MV is a Software Development Engineer in Test at Signify, where his work is shifting from writing tests by hand to building the systems that write, run and heal them.

Before Signify he spent seven years as a Senior Analyst at Harman International, owning test automation, API testing and a data-lake project, and building the Cypress and Karate frameworks his teams relied on. He started out at Snapooh Media, a startup where he picked up a little of every layer of the stack, and holds a B.E. in Information Science from Visvesvaraya Technological University.

His open-source work includes AgenticPw, an AI teammate that writes Playwright tests and proposes verified fixes (or honest refusals) for failing ones, and luma-browser, a CLI and daemon that lets scripts drive real browsers from a QuickJS sandbox. He writes about test automation and AI agents at jagannathareddy.github.io.
